# Guitar.io 1.6.0

Cloud sync is part of the free plan. Free accounts support five saved songs/shared links and 150 MiB of cloud storage. Premium remains AUD 5/month or 50/year, with unlimited song count within 1 GiB, automatic sections, synchronised YouTube, online metadata/GP discovery and additional beta access. No advertising.

Saved libraries are preserved when access changes. Choose active songs after a downgrade; archived entries remain stored/exportable. Add music links as pending songs and attach a GP file without using a second slot. Complete originals and attachments download for offline use.

Rhythm stems/beams are explicitly rendered, with source durations preserved. Section colours can be disabled. Basic Learn remains free and labelled beta. Guitar deletion, concise help and update alerts are retained.

This source also introduces the React Native Apple app and Xcode handoff, a private owner dashboard with MFA and audited grants, feedback, opt-in aggregate usage reporting and education foundations.

Hosted services require setup. Native iOS compilation, migration, sound, sharing and purchase checks must be completed on Mac/iPhone. See VALIDATION-v1.6.0.md and IOS_REACT_NATIVE_HANDOFF.md.
