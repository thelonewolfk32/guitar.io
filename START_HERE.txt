Guitar.io V1.6.0 local test candidate

Read VALIDATION-v1.6.0.md for checks and remaining work.
No Supabase project or live payment services are configured yet.

Windows: extract the full ZIP, then open Guitar.io.exe.
Apple Silicon Mac: extract the Mac ZIP, run Prepare and open Guitar.io.command once, then open Guitar.io.app.

Free: local/cloud sync, five saved songs or links, manual GP files and Learn (beta).
Premium: unlimited song count within 1 GiB and automatic tools; AUD $5/month or $50/year.
No advertising. Existing songs and source files are preserved after downgrade.
Choose active entries in Account & plans. Archived songs remain exportable.

Use Share a song to save a Spotify, YouTube or Apple Music link.
Attach your chosen GP file to complete the same entry.
Player settings includes a persistent Section colours switch.
Guitar settings includes Delete guitar, keeping songs.
Help includes feedback. Usage counts are optional and off by default.

Update both paired computers before testing Device sync.
Sync transfers original GP files, artwork and audio attachments for offline use.
Keep source devices awake until complete; missing source files and quota failures are reported.

Help (?) > version number opens Updates. A ! indicates a newer release.
Check for updates downloads and verifies; Install update applies it when ready.
Active saves/imports and sync finish before installation. Personal app data is separate.
